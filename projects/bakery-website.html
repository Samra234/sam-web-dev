Trust Bakers & Sweets Website

Project Overview & Objective

Trust Bakers & Sweets needed a modern, high-converting digital storefront to showcase their gourmet cakes, fresh morning pastries, and traditional sweets. The goal was to replace phone call order bottlenecks with a streamlined online catalog where customers could customize cake orders and dispatch inquiries directly to WhatsApp.

Key Solutions Delivered

  • ✓ Custom Cake Reservation Engine: Interactive forms allowing clients to pick flavor, weight (lbs), and custom inscription.
  • ✓ Instant WhatsApp Dispatch: One-click checkout button formatting order details directly into WhatsApp.
  • ✓ Lightning-Fast Mobile Loading: 100/100 Google Lighthouse score on Netlify CDN hosting.
  • ✓ Visual Dessert Showcase: High-resolution grid layout highlighting daily baked goods.
