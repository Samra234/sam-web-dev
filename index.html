 HOW I WORK

          
Structured 7-Step Development Process

          
From initial discussion to post-launch support, I ensure full transparency and timely delivery.

        


        

          

            01
            
DISCOVER

            
Discuss your business goal, target audience, & specs.

          


          

            02
            
PLAN

            
Define site structure, tech stack, & project scope.

          


          

            03
            
DESIGN

            
Create modern, responsive UI/UX visual wireframes.

          


          

            04
            
DEVELOP

            
Write clean, high-performance HTML/CSS/JS or Kotlin code.

          


          

            05
            
TEST

            
Cross-browser, speed, SEO & mobile responsiveness audit.

          


          

            06
            
LAUNCH

            
Deploy to Netlify / Play Store with SSL & domain setup.

          


          

            07
            
SUPPORT

            
Provide post-launch maintenance, security, & updates.
